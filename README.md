### 🧭 About me

Angular is my main tool, but I'm not limited to the frontend. I build applications end-to-end — from requirements and Figma prototypes, through UI and business logic, integrations with external services and AI, all the way to automated testing and deployment.

- 🏥 Currently maintaining and evolving a **HIS (Hospital/Human Information System)** in Angular
- 🤖 Wiring internal tools into **AI agentic workflows** (MCP / skills) on top of local LLMs, in **Go**
- 🧪 Own the automated testing setup (Cypress) and mentor teammates through onboarding & code review
- 🎓 MSc in Computer Science (AI specialization) — Adam Mickiewicz University, Poznań
- 🌱 Always tinkering: woodworking, smart electronics, and running local AI models at home
